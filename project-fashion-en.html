01 Project Overview
Category Fashion Campaign
Client Atelier KSA
Services Art Direction, Photography, Film
Duration 3 Weeks

A seasonal campaign built around three looks, three locations, and a single editorial thread. We handled art direction end to end — casting, styling liaison, location scouting, and shoot supervision.

The delivery included a hero film, vertical cutdowns for social, and a print-ready lookbook. Each piece was graded to share a family feel without becoming repetitive.
