Gourmet Restaurant

Highlighting fine dining dishes with incredible quality and mood.

01 Project Overview
Category Food & Hospitality
Client Gourmet Riyadh
Services Food Photography, Promo Video
Duration 2 Weeks

A full visual identity refresh for a fine dining concept. We shot the entire menu in-studio and on-site, building a library that works for print, web, and delivery platforms.

The look leans into warm, low-key lighting and tight macro detail — positioning each dish as the subject of its own short film. A 60-second promo tied the stills together for launch.
