Luxury Perfume Launch

Cinematic photo session reflecting luxury and identity.

01 Project Overview
Category Product Launch
Client Maison Scent
Services Product Photography, Launch Film
Duration 2 Weeks

A launch campaign for a niche fragrance house. The creative brief centred on restraint — a single prop, considered light, and a colour palette drawn directly from the bottle itself.

We produced a family of hero stills alongside a 30-second launch film, both engineered to sit comfortably inside the brand's existing editorial identity while opening up new campaign directions.
